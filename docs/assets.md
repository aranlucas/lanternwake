# Original artwork

`public/assets/garden.png` and `public/assets/atlas.png` were generated for this
prototype with the built-in Image Gen tool, under the delegated art permission.
They use an original cream-hooded lantern keeper, no franchise characters or
stock photographs. No AI provider is invoked by the shipped application.

`docs/concept.png` is the pre-implementation full-screen design reference.
The standalone garden was generated by removing reference UI and game objects.
The transparent 4×3 atlas contains keeper, tile, moon pads, closed/open gate,
unlit/lit bell, star pads, and dawn pads. The raster originals are unchanged;
Phaser extracts frames at runtime and applies echo tints and movement tweens.

Tiny HUD arrows, lantern, journal, undo, and audio symbols are code-native SVG
icons. Sound cues are short Web Audio sine-note sequences authored in code.
There is no downloaded music, voice recording, or third-party character art.
